# Modern Green Lantern (2004-2026)

Green Lantern from *Green Lantern: Rebirth* (2004) through the issues on sale as of October 14, 2026. There's one combined list, one list per era, and a separate list for out-of-continuity books.

| List | Issues |
|---|---|
| 00 - Complete | 823 |
| 01 - Geoff Johns Era (2004-2011) | 277 |
| 02 - New 52 (2011-2016) | 290 |
| 03 - Rebirth (2016-2019) | 112 |
| 04 - Morrison & Thorne (2019-2023) | 57 |
| 05 - Dawn of DC & All In (2022-2026) | 87 |
| XX - Out of Continuity | 76 |

The Complete list is lists 01-05 joined together. The year ranges in the file names come from cover dates.

## How it was built

- Lists 01-04 start from the lists in `../Reddit` (05-08) and keep their order. I compared them against the lists in `../CBRO` to catch Lantern issues they were missing.
- List 05 is `DC/Creator Runs/[DC] [2022-2026] Green Lantern by Jeremy Adams.cbl` plus *Alan Scott: The Green Lantern* #1-6 and *Tales of the Green Lantern Corps: Guy Gardner* #1. That list follows the CBRO order for Green Lantern (2023) and Green Lantern Corps (2025), with GL #13-15 and *Absolute Power: Task Force VII* #3 added.
- Every ComicVine ID was checked against the ComicVine API on 2026-10-14 for volume, issue number and cover year.

## Changes from the Reddit lists

- **Added:**
  - *Blue Beetle* (2006) #20, a Sinestro Corps War chapter
  - *Suicide Squad* (1987) #67, which completes the Rise of the Black Lanterns one-shots
  - *Infinity Man and the Forever People* #4-6, the Godhead chapters
  - *Green Lantern 80th Anniversary 100-Page Super Spectacular*
- **Removed:** *Green Arrow* (2011) #30, the "Outsiders War" chapter, which has no Lantern content. It's also removed from `../Reddit/Green Lantern 07 - DC Rebirth.cbl` in the same PR.

## Scope

- **Events:** Lantern events are included in full. Other DC events are only included where Lanterns are central. This matches the Reddit lists: Convergence #1-3 only, the Flashpoint Lantern minis without Flashpoint #1-5, and Dark Nights: Metal limited to *Batman: The Dawnbreaker*.
- **Other Corps and character books** are included: Red Lanterns, Sinestro, Larfleeze, Threshold, The Omega Men (2015), Far Sector, John Stewart: The Emerald Knight and Alan Scott: The Green Lantern.
- **Out of Continuity** covers:
  - Elseworlds: Earth One, Green Lantern: Dark, Absolute Green Lantern and Tales from the Dark Multiverse
  - YA graphic novels: Legacy, Alliance and Unearthed
  - Media tie-ins: the movie prequels, the animated series comic and Smallville: Lantern
  - Crossovers with other franchises: Star Trek, Planet of the Apes, Space Ghost and Huckleberry Hound

  Each book is kept together, in the order they started.
- Trades, reprints and facsimile editions are left out.
