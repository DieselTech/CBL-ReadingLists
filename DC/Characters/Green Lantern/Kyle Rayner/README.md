# Kyle Rayner (1994-2026)

Kyle Rayner from *Emerald Twilight* (1994), where he gets the last Green Lantern ring, through *Green Lantern* (2023) #39, the issues on sale as of October 14, 2026. It's one list of 317 issues.

It's the CBL version of the [Kyle Rayner reading order](https://www.greenlanternwatch.com/comics/reading-orders/kyle-rayner-reading-order) on Green Lantern Watch, which has a note on each entry.

## Scope

- Only stories where Kyle is a lead or co-lead. It started from every issue ComicVine lists him in and kept the ones he drives.
- Chapters from other series are included when Kyle co-stars, like his crossovers with Green Arrow and the Flash.
- Left out: his years on the JLA, Countdown and the big events where he's one of many heroes.
- *Emerald Twilight* (Green Lantern #48-50) is the one exception. It's Hal Jordan's story, but it's where Kyle gets the ring.
- Issues of his own books that belong to someone else are skipped, like the Jade issues of Green Lantern (#108-111) and the John Stewart issue (#156).
- For the Sinestro Corps War, only Kyle's chapters are included. The full crossover is in `../Reddit/Green Lantern 05 - Geoff Johns Era.cbl`.
- Trades, reprints and facsimile editions are left out.

## How it was built

- Every ComicVine ID was checked against ComicVine data pulled on 2026-10-14 for volume, issue number and cover year.
- The year range in the file name comes from cover dates.
